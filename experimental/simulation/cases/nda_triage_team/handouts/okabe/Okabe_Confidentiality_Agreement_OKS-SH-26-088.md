# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

Okabe Lifestyle Retail (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "Okabe") and Hangzhou Yunzhan Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "Yunzhan") hereby enter into this Confidentiality Agreement (hereinafter this "Agreement") as follows, in connection with Okabe's evaluation of Yunzhan's member-engagement software for Okabe's stores in mainland China.

**Article 1 (Purpose)**
The purpose of this Agreement is to set out the obligations of Okabe and Yunzhan (each a "Party") concerning the handling of confidential information exchanged between them for the evaluation described above (the "Purpose").

**Article 2 (Confidential Information)**
1. "Confidential Information" means technical, business and other information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") for the Purpose, in documents, electronic data or other tangible form marked as confidential, or disclosed orally and specified as confidential at the time of disclosure and confirmed in writing within thirty (30) days thereafter.
2. Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, the following information shall not be Confidential Information:
(1) information that was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure;
(2) information that enters the public domain after disclosure through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(3) information that the Receiving Party already lawfully possessed at the time of disclosure;
(4) information that the Receiving Party lawfully obtains from a third party with no duty of confidentiality; and
(5) information that the Receiving Party develops independently without relying on the Confidential Information.

**Article 3 (Obligation of Confidentiality)**
1. The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, and shall manage the Confidential Information with the care of a prudent manager.
2. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its and its affiliates' officers, employees, contractors and outside professionals who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that the Receiving Party causes them to observe obligations equivalent to those under this Agreement and is responsible for their compliance.
3. If disclosure of Confidential Information is required by laws and regulations or by an order of a court or public authority, the Receiving Party may disclose it to the minimum extent required, after notifying the Disclosing Party in advance to the extent permitted by law.

**Article 4 (Restriction on Use)**
The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information for the Purpose alone; use for any other purpose, including to train or improve an artificial intelligence or machine learning model, is prohibited.

**Article 5 (Personal Information)**
The Parties shall not exchange personal information, including the personal information of Okabe's store members, under this Agreement. If the handling of personal information becomes necessary, the Parties shall separately conclude a written agreement on such handling in accordance with applicable laws.

**Article 6 (Return and Disposal)**
Upon request of the Disclosing Party or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or dispose of the Confidential Information. Copies stored in backup systems in the ordinary course of business need not be disposed of, but shall remain subject to the obligations of this Agreement until they are deleted.

**Article 7 (Term of Validity)**
1. This Agreement shall be valid for one (1) year from the date of execution. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other Party one (1) month's prior written notice.
2. Articles 3, 4 and 6 shall continue to apply for two (2) years following the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

**Article 8 (Know-how and Experience)**
This Agreement does not prevent the officers or employees of either Party from making use of the general knowledge and experience they gain in the course of the Purpose, provided that such use does not involve the use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information.

**Article 9 (No Grant of Rights)**
No right or license under any patent, utility model, design, trademark, copyright or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party shall be granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement.

**Article 10 (Injunction)**
In the event of a breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, the other Party may seek an injunction or other interim measures in addition to any claim for damages.

**Article 11 (Export Control)**
When disclosing or receiving technical information under this Agreement, each Party shall observe the export control laws and regulations that apply to it.

**Article 12 (Governing Law and Dispute Resolution)**
1. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
2. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission (CIETAC) for arbitration in Beijing in accordance with its arbitration rules in effect at the time of applying for arbitration. The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon both Parties.

**Article 13 (Miscellaneous)**
1. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter.
2. This Agreement may be amended only by written agreement signed or sealed by both Parties.
3. Neither Party shall assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement to a third party without the prior written consent of the other Party.
4. The name or trademarks of the other Party may not be used in any publicity unless that Party has given its prior written consent.
5. This Agreement is made in English in two (2) counterparts, and each Party shall retain one counterpart after signature or sealing. Electronic seals are acceptable.
