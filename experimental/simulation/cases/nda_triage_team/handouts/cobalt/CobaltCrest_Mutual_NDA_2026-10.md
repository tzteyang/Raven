# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on ____________, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Cobalt Crest Commerce, LLC, a California limited liability company located at 2150 North First Street, Suite 450, San Jose, California 95131 ("Cobalt Crest"), and Yunzhan Technology (Hong Kong) Limited, a Hong Kong company located at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("Yunzhan").

**1. Purpose.** The parties are considering an arrangement under which Cobalt Crest would resell and support Yunzhan's loyalty software for specialty retailers in Northern California and the Pacific Northwest (the "Opportunity"). This Agreement governs the confidential information the parties exchange while they evaluate the Opportunity.

**2. Definitions.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information that one party (the "Disclosing Party") provides to the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Opportunity, in any form, which is marked "confidential" or "proprietary" or which, given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, the Receiving Party should reasonably understand to be confidential. Confidential Information includes product roadmaps, release schedules, pricing, margin structures, sales pipelines and software documentation.

**3. Obligations.** The Receiving Party will (a) use Confidential Information only to evaluate and pursue the Opportunity; (b) disclose it only to its employees, officers, contractors and advisors, and those of its affiliates, who need to know it for the Opportunity and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement; and (c) protect it using the same care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care. The Receiving Party is responsible for the acts and omissions of the persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

**4. Exclusions.** The obligations of this Agreement do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) the Receiving Party lawfully knew before receiving it from the Disclosing Party, free of any confidentiality obligation; (c) the Receiving Party lawfully obtains from a third party without a confidentiality obligation; or (d) the Receiving Party independently develops without using the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**5. Legally Required Disclosure.** If a court, regulator or law requires the Receiving Party to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party may do so after giving the Disclosing Party prompt notice, where the law permits notice, and will disclose only what is required.

**6. Residuals.** Each party shall be free to use for any purpose the residuals resulting from access to or work with the Confidential Information of the other party. The term "residuals" means information in non-tangible form which may be retained in the unaided memory of persons who have had access to the Confidential Information, including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques contained therein. Neither party shall have any obligation to limit or restrict the assignment of such persons or to pay royalties for any work resulting from the use of residuals.

**7. Return of Materials.** Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party will promptly return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. The Receiving Party is not required to delete copies stored in its routine electronic backups, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

**8. Term.** This Agreement runs for two (2) years from the Effective Date; either party may end it sooner by giving thirty (30) days' written notice. As to Confidential Information received before this Agreement ends, the Receiving Party's obligations continue for five (5) years after it ends.

**9. Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license under any patent, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement.

**10. Disclaimers.** All Confidential Information is supplied "AS IS", with no warranty of any kind. Nothing here obliges either party to pursue the Opportunity or to sign any further agreement.

**11. Remedies.** Each party agrees that breaching this Agreement could cause the other party harm that money cannot repair, so the other party may ask a court for injunctive relief on top of any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**12. Independent Activities.** Each party understands that the other may already be developing, or may later develop or obtain from third parties, products or information resembling the Confidential Information; nothing in this Agreement stops it from doing so, provided that it does not use or disclose the other party's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

**13. Miscellaneous.** This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. Neither party may assign it without the other party's prior written consent, except to a successor of its business. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder will continue in effect. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

**14. Choice of Law; Forum.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Santa Clara County, California.
