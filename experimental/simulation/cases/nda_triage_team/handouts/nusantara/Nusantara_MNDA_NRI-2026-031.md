# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made between:

**NUSANTARA RETAIL INTEGRATION SDN. BHD.** (Registration No. 201801029384 (1290112-K)), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its business address at Level 12, Menara Rimbun, Jalan Tun Razak, 50400 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia ("NRI"); and

**YUNZHAN TECHNOLOGY (HONG KONG) LIMITED**, a company incorporated in Hong Kong with its registered office at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("Yunzhan"),

(each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties").

**RECITALS**

A. NRI provides point-of-sale integration services to retailers in Malaysia.

B. The Parties intend to connect NRI's integration hub with Yunzhan's member-engagement platform for two pilot retail brands in Malaysia, and to evaluate a wider reseller arrangement (the "Project").

**1. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Project, whether before or after the date of this Agreement and in any form, including technical data, API specifications, system credentials, pricing, business plans, customer data, end-user data and personal data, whether or not marked as confidential.

1.2 "Representatives" means the directors, officers, employees, subcontractors and professional advisers of a Party and of its related corporations.

**2. OBLIGATIONS OF CONFIDENTIALITY**

2.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and use it only for the Project.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives who need to know it for the Project and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and with no less care than it applies to its own confidential information.

**3. EXCEPTIONS**

3.1 The obligations in Clause 2 shall not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public knowledge other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was already lawfully known to the Receiving Party, free of any obligation of confidence, when it was disclosed;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party who is not bound by any obligation of confidence; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

**4. DISCLOSURE REQUIRED BY LAW**

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or by any court, regulatory or governmental authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior notice and limits the disclosure to what is required.

**5. RETURN OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

5.1 On written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within thirty (30) days, except for copies held in backup systems that are deleted in accordance with the Receiving Party's normal retention practices and which remain subject to this Agreement until deleted.

**6. NO LICENCE**

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No right or licence is granted to the Receiving Party in respect of any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except as necessary for the Project.

**7. TERM AND SURVIVAL**

7.1 This Agreement shall be effective from the date of the last signature below and shall continue for one (1) year unless terminated earlier by either Party giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement shall survive for a period of forty-eight (48) months after the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

**8. NO WARRANTY**

8.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party is obliged by this Agreement to proceed with the Project or any other transaction.

**9. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF**

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that monetary damages may not be a sufficient remedy for unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information and that the other Party shall be entitled, without waiving any other rights or remedies, to seek injunctive or other equitable relief.

**10. NOTICES**

10.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by email with a copy by courier to the addresses stated above.

**11. PUBLICITY**

11.1 Neither Party shall publicise the Project or use the other Party's name or logo without the other Party's prior written consent.

**12. GENERAL**

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter.

12.2 Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

12.4 Nothing in this Agreement constitutes a partnership, joint venture or agency between the Parties.

12.5 If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

12.6 Neither Party shall, during the term of this Agreement and for eighteen (18) months after its expiry or termination, directly or indirectly solicit or entice away, or attempt to solicit or entice away, any employee of the other Party.

12.7 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and signed electronically.

**13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Singapore.

13.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre in accordance with its arbitration rules for the time being in force. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore, the tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.
