# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on ______________ 2026

**between**

**Lotus Gate Hospitality Group Limited**, a company registered in the Macao Special Administrative Region with its principal office at Avenida da Praia Grande No. 429, 15th Floor, Macao ("**Lotus Gate**"); and

**Yunzhan Technology (Hong Kong) Limited**, a company incorporated in Hong Kong with its registered office at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("**Yunzhan**").

**Recitals**

(A) Lotus Gate operates hotels and the retail outlets located in them, and runs a guest membership programme across its properties.

(B) Lotus Gate is evaluating Yunzhan's member-engagement software for its membership programme and retail outlets (the "**Evaluation**"), and the parties will exchange confidential information for that purpose.

**1. Definitions**

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by either party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other party (the "**Receiving Party**") for the Evaluation, in any form, that is identified as confidential when disclosed or that a reasonable business person would treat as confidential.

1.2 "**Representatives**" means a party's directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers and those of its affiliated companies.

**2. Undertakings**

2.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Evaluation and shall not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Evaluation and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

2.2 The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives and shall protect the Confidential Information with at least reasonable care.

**3. Exceptions**

3.1 The undertakings in clause 2 do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party, without obligation of confidence, before it was disclosed;

(c) is lawfully disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party without restriction; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**4. Disclosure required by law**

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, regulation or the order of a competent court or authority, provided that, to the extent lawful, it informs the Disclosing Party in advance and limits the disclosure to what is required.

**5. Personal data**

5.1 No personal data of Lotus Gate's guests or members shall be disclosed to Yunzhan during the Evaluation. Any future disclosure of personal data shall be governed by a separate written agreement that complies with applicable data protection laws.

**6. Return and destruction**

6.1 Within thirty (30) days after the Disclosing Party asks in writing, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy that party's Confidential Information. Back-up copies made automatically by its IT systems may be kept until they are overwritten, and stay subject to this Agreement until then.

**7. Term**

7.1 This Agreement is in force for two (2) years from the date above; either party may end it sooner by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 For thirty-six (36) months after this Agreement expires or is terminated, the Receiving Party remains bound by clauses 2 to 6.

**8. Property, licence and warranty**

8.1 The Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence or right in respect of the Disclosing Party's intellectual property.

8.2 Confidential Information is disclosed without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither party is obliged to enter into any further agreement.

**9. Equitable relief**

9.1 Each party acknowledges that damages may not be a sufficient remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the non-breaching party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief or other equitable remedies.

**10. Announcements**

10.1 No announcement of the Evaluation, and no use of the other party's name, logo or trade marks, may be made without that party's prior written consent.

**11. Enforcement**

11.1 A party that does not enforce, or delays in enforcing, any right under this Agreement keeps that right.

11.2 If proceedings are brought to enforce this Agreement, the party that prevails is entitled to be reimbursed by the other party for the reasonable legal fees and costs of those proceedings.

**12. General**

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter.

12.2 Any amendment of this Agreement shall be in writing and signed by both parties.

12.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

12.4 Notices shall be given in writing to the addresses set out above.

12.5 This Agreement is executed in English. If it is translated into Chinese or Portuguese, the English text shall prevail.

12.6 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

**13. Governing law and arbitration**

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the HKSAR.

13.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force when the Notice of Arbitration is submitted. The seat of arbitration shall be Hong Kong, and the arbitration shall be conducted in English.
