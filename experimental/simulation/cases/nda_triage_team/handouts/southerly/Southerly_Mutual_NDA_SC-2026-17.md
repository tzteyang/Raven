# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**Date:** ______________ 2026

**Parties**

1. **Southerly Commerce Pty Ltd** (ACN 642 118 901) of Level 9, 60 Margaret Street, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia (**Southerly**).

2. **Yunzhan Technology (Hong Kong) Limited** of Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong (**Yunzhan**).

**Background**

A. Southerly sells and implements retail software for supermarket and specialty retail chains in Australia and New Zealand.

B. Southerly and Yunzhan wish to explore a distribution relationship for Yunzhan's member-engagement platform in Australia and New Zealand (the **Project**), and will exchange Confidential Information for that purpose.

**Operative provisions**

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this agreement:

**Confidential Information** of a party means all information, in any form, relating to that party's business, products, technology, pricing, customers or plans that is disclosed by or for that party (the **Discloser**) to the other party (the **Recipient**) in connection with the Project and that is identified as confidential or is by its nature confidential;

**Personnel** of a party means its officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers and those of its related bodies corporate.

**2. Confidentiality**

2.1 The Recipient must keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Project.

2.2 The Recipient may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information only to its Personnel who need to know it for the Project and who are bound by obligations of confidence no less stringent than those in this agreement, and the Recipient is responsible for any breach of those obligations by its Personnel.

2.3 The Recipient must take reasonable steps, and at least the steps it takes for its own confidential information, to protect the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**3. Exclusions**

3.1 Clause 2 does not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this agreement;

(b) the Recipient lawfully possessed before the Discloser disclosed it, free of any obligation of confidence;

(c) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party who is entitled to disclose it without restriction; or

(d) the Recipient develops independently without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**4. Disclosure required by law**

4.1 Where the law, a court, a regulator or the listing rules of a stock exchange require it, the Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to that extent, if, where lawful, it notifies the Discloser promptly and discloses only what is required.

**5. Personal information**

5.1 The parties do not intend to disclose personal information to each other under this agreement. Any disclosure of personal information in connection with the Project must first be covered by a separate written data processing agreement.

**6. Security of information**

6.1 The Recipient must keep the Discloser's Confidential Information in secure storage and may copy it only so far as the Project reasonably requires.

**7. Return or destruction**

7.1 On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient must within thirty (30) days return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not require the Recipient to purge copies retained in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided those copies remain subject to this agreement until they are overwritten.

**8. No licence and no warranty**

8.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser, and nothing in this agreement grants the Recipient any licence or other right in any intellectual property of the Discloser.

8.2 The Discloser gives no warranty that its Confidential Information is accurate or complete, and neither party is obliged to proceed with the Project.

**9. Term**

9.1 This agreement starts on the date it is signed by both parties and ends two (2) years after that date, unless either party ends it earlier by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.2 Clauses 2 to 7 survive the expiry or termination of this agreement and bind each party in perpetuity.

**10. Injunctive relief**

10.1 Each party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this agreement, and that each party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to restrain an actual or threatened breach.

**11. Announcements**

11.1 A party must not make any public announcement about the Project, or use the other party's name or logo, without the other party's prior written consent.

**12. Notices**

12.1 Notices under this agreement must be in writing and sent by email to the address a party nominates for notices, with a copy delivered to its address set out above.

**13. Assignment**

13.1 A party may not assign or novate its rights or obligations under this agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

**14. Governing law and disputes**

14.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of the HKSAR.

14.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force when the Notice of Arbitration is submitted. The seat of arbitration shall be Hong Kong and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

**15. General**

15.1 This agreement is the entire agreement of the parties about its subject matter.

15.2 This agreement may only be varied in writing signed by both parties.

15.3 A waiver of a right under this agreement must be in writing and is effective only to the extent stated.

15.4 If a provision of this agreement is invalid or unenforceable, it is to be read down or severed to the extent necessary, and the rest of this agreement remains in force.

15.5 Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties.

15.6 This agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically.

15.7 While this agreement is in force and for twelve (12) months after it ends, neither party may, without the other party's written consent, directly or indirectly induce or encourage any of the other party's employees who worked on the Project to leave their employment.
