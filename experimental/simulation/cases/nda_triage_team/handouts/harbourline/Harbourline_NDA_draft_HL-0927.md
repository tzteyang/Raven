# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Agreement** is made on the date of the last signature below

**Between**

(1) **HARBOURLINE DIGITAL LIMITED** (Company No. 2874105), a company incorporated in Hong Kong whose registered office is at 18/F, Millennium Industrial Tower, 61 Hoi Yuen Road, Kwun Tong, Kowloon, Hong Kong ("**Harbourline**"); and

(2) **YUNZHAN TECHNOLOGY (HONG KONG) LIMITED**, a company incorporated in Hong Kong whose registered office is at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("**Yunzhan**").

**Whereas** the parties are discussing an arrangement under which Harbourline would resell and implement Yunzhan's member-engagement platform for retailers in Hong Kong and Macau (the "**Proposed Arrangement**"), and each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other for that purpose.

**It is agreed** as follows:

**1. Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in Hong Kong;

"**Confidential Information**" means all information, in whatever form, relating to the business, products, technology, pricing, customers or plans of a party (the "**Discloser**") that is disclosed to the other party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the Proposed Arrangement and that is marked or described as confidential, or that would reasonably be regarded as confidential;

"**Representatives**" means, in relation to a party, its directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers, and those of its group companies.

1.2 Clause headings do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**2. Confidentiality undertakings**

2.1 The Recipient shall hold the Discloser's Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any person except as permitted by clause 3.

2.2 The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the purpose of evaluating and negotiating the Proposed Arrangement.

2.3 The Recipient shall take at least the same care to protect the Discloser's Confidential Information as it takes to protect its own confidential information of a similar kind, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

**3. Permitted disclosure**

3.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Proposed Arrangement, provided that each of them is bound by duties of confidentiality no less onerous than this Agreement. The Recipient shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

3.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, by any court of competent jurisdiction or by any governmental or regulatory authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt written notice and discloses only what is required.

**4. Exceptions**

4.1 Clauses 2 and 3 do not apply to information which:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession before it was disclosed by the Discloser, without any obligation of confidence;

(c) is received by the Recipient from a third party who was lawfully entitled to disclose it without restriction; or

(d) is developed by or for the Recipient independently of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**5. Return and destruction**

5.1 At any time on the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall within fifteen (15) Business Days return or destroy all documents and materials containing the Discloser's Confidential Information.

5.2 Clause 5.1 does not require the Recipient to delete copies held in automatic electronic back-up or archiving systems, provided that such copies are not accessed and remain subject to this Agreement.

**6. Ownership and no licence**

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.

6.2 Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence or right in respect of the Discloser's intellectual property, other than the right to use the Confidential Information for the purpose in clause 2.2.

6.3 Nothing in this Agreement shall restrict either party from using, for any purpose, information (including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques) that is retained in the unaided memory of its Representatives who had access to the other party's Confidential Information, and neither party shall be obliged to restrict the work assignments of such Representatives.

**7. Duration**

7.1 This Agreement shall continue for two (2) years from the date of the last signature below, unless terminated earlier by either party on one (1) month's written notice.

7.2 The obligations of each party under clauses 2 to 5 shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and shall continue in respect of any item of Confidential Information until that information becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement.

**8. Remedies**

8.1 Each party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the other party shall be entitled to seek an injunction or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach.

**9. No representation**

9.1 Neither party makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information.

9.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to enter into the Proposed Arrangement.

**10. Personal data**

10.1 Neither party shall disclose personal data (as defined in the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)) to the other under this Agreement unless the parties have first agreed separate written terms for its handling.

**11. Announcements**

11.1 Neither party shall announce or publicise the Proposed Arrangement, or use the other party's name or logo, without the other party's prior written consent.

**12. General**

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter.

12.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

12.3 Neither party may assign or transfer any of its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

12.4 Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership or agency relationship between the parties.

12.5 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Ordinance (Cap. 623) to enforce any of its terms.

12.6 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and signed electronically.

**13. Governing law and dispute resolution**

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Hong Kong SAR.

13.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre (HKIAC) under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force when the Notice of Arbitration is submitted. The seat of arbitration shall be Hong Kong SAR. There shall be one arbitrator, and the arbitration shall be conducted in English.
