# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**THIS AGREEMENT** is dated ______________ 2026

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **LINDQVIST STORE ANALYTICS (SHANGHAI) CO., LTD.**, a wholly foreign-owned enterprise established in the People's Republic of China with its registered address at Room 1608, No. 1018 Changning Road, Changning District, Shanghai ("**Lindqvist**"); and

(2) **HANGZHOU YUNZHAN TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD.**, a company established in the People's Republic of China with its registered address at Yunzhan Tower, No. 11 Xiyuan 8th Road, Xihu District, Hangzhou ("**Yunzhan**"),

each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**".

**BACKGROUND**

The parties intend to evaluate a combined offering of Lindqvist's in-store traffic analytics and Yunzhan's member-engagement software for European fashion brands that operate stores in mainland China, and for that purpose each party expects to share confidential information with the other.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions**

1.1 **Affiliate**: any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership of more than fifty per cent of the voting interests.

1.2 **Confidential Information**: all information of a confidential nature disclosed by or on behalf of a party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, which is marked as confidential or, if disclosed orally or by demonstration, is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure and summarised in writing within thirty (30) days.

1.3 **Purpose**: the evaluation and discussion of the combined offering described in the Background.

1.4 **Representatives**: a party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers.

**2. Confidentiality undertakings**

2.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Purpose.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that it informs them of its confidential nature and ensures that they are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less strict than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is liable for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall apply to the Confidential Information no lower degree of care than it applies to its own confidential information, and in any case reasonable care.

2.4 Neither party shall use the other party's Confidential Information to train, fine-tune or otherwise improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model.

**3. Exceptions**

The obligations in clause 2 shall not apply to any information which:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party, free of any obligation of confidence, before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is lawfully obtained by the Receiving Party from a third party who is not under an obligation of confidence in respect of it; or

(d) is independently developed by or on behalf of the Receiving Party without access to or use of the Confidential Information.

**4. Disclosure required by law**

A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, by any governmental or regulatory authority or by a court of competent jurisdiction, provided that, to the extent it is legally permitted to do so, it gives the other party as much notice of the disclosure as possible and discloses no more than is required.

**5. Personal information**

The parties do not intend to exchange personal information under this Agreement. The in-store traffic information that Lindqvist may share for the Purpose consists only of aggregated and anonymous visitor counts. If either party later needs to share personal information, the parties shall first enter into a separate written agreement governing its processing.

**6. Return and destruction**

On the Disclosing Party's written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, save that it may keep copies that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems and cannot reasonably be deleted. Any such copies remain subject to this Agreement until they are overwritten in the ordinary course of business.

**7. Duration**

7.1 This Agreement starts on the date stated above and remains in force for two (2) years, unless either party terminates it earlier by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 The obligations in clauses 2 to 6 continue for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement, except that they continue in respect of any trade secret for as long as it remains a trade secret under applicable law.

**8. Knowledge and skills**

Nothing in this Agreement prevents a party's personnel from using the general knowledge, skills and experience that they acquire in the course of the Purpose, provided that this clause does not permit the use or disclosure of any Confidential Information of the other party.

**9. No licence, no obligation, no warranty**

9.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any licence or other right in respect of any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, other than the right to use the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

9.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to proceed with any transaction, and each party remains free to work with third parties on similar projects, provided that it does not use the other party's Confidential Information.

9.3 Confidential Information is provided "as is" and without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

**10. Equitable relief**

Each party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that either party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief or specific performance for any threatened or actual breach.

**11. Governing law and arbitration**

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China (for the purpose of this Agreement excluding the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macau Special Administrative Region and Taiwan).

11.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the Shanghai International Arbitration Center for arbitration in Shanghai in accordance with its arbitration rules in effect when the application for arbitration is submitted. The arbitral award is final and binding on both parties.

**12. General**

12.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter.

12.2 No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

12.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, save to a successor to all or substantially all of its relevant business.

12.4 Neither party shall make any public announcement about the Purpose, or use the other party's name or logo, without the other party's prior written consent.

12.5 Notices shall be sent by email and courier to the addresses set out above or to such other address as a party notifies in writing.

12.6 This Agreement is executed in English. It may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature or company seal, each of which is an original.
