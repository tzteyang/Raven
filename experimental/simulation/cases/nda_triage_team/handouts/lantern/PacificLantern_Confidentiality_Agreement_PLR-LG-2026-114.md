# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**DATE:** ______________ 2026

**PARTIES**

1. **PACIFIC LANTERN RETAIL HOLDINGS LIMITED**, a company incorporated in Hong Kong with its registered office at 30/F, Lantern Centre, 9 Queen's Road Central, Central, Hong Kong ("**PLR**"); and

2. **YUNZHAN TECHNOLOGY (HONG KONG) LIMITED**, a company incorporated in Hong Kong with its registered office at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("**Yunzhan**").

**BACKGROUND**

PLR operates department stores and lifestyle stores in Hong Kong and Macau and is assessing Yunzhan's member-engagement software as a replacement for its existing loyalty system (the "**Evaluation**"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other in the course of the Evaluation.

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or general holiday in Hong Kong;

"**Confidential Information**" means any information of a confidential nature, in any form, that is disclosed by a party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Evaluation, including information relating to store operations, membership programmes, product designs, system architecture, security measures and commercial terms;

"**Group**" means, in relation to a party, that party and its subsidiaries and holding companies from time to time.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only.

**2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS**

2.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information secret and shall use it solely for the purposes of the Evaluation.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to the directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers of its Group who need to know it for the Evaluation and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality equivalent to those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any failure by those persons to comply with such obligations.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the same measures it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in any event no less than reasonable measures.

**3. EXCEPTIONS**

3.1 Clause 2 does not apply to information which the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is in, or enters, the public domain other than by breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in its possession before disclosure by the Disclosing Party and not subject to any obligation of confidence;

(c) was lawfully received from a third party free to disclose it; or

(d) was independently developed by it without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE**

4.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of any stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent permitted by law, it notifies the Disclosing Party promptly and discloses only the information that it is required to disclose.

**5. PERSONAL DATA**

5.1 The parties do not expect to disclose personal data to each other in the course of the Evaluation. No personal data shall be disclosed under this Agreement unless the parties have first entered into a separate written data processing agreement.

**6. RETURN OF INFORMATION**

6.1 On the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall within twenty (20) Business Days return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except that copies held on automatic electronic back-up systems need not be deleted but shall remain subject to this Agreement until overwritten.

**7. DURATION**

7.1 This Agreement shall continue until the earlier of (a) two (2) years from its date, or (b) termination by either party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 The obligations in clauses 2 to 6 shall continue for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement, save that obligations in respect of trade secrets shall continue for as long as the relevant information remains a trade secret.

**8. AUDIT AND INSPECTION**

8.1 Each party (the "**Auditing Party**") may, on not less than ten (10) Business Days' written notice and not more than once in any twelve-month period, appoint its internal auditors or an independent auditor to inspect the records, information systems and premises of the other party to the extent reasonably necessary to verify the other party's compliance with this Agreement.

8.2 The audited party shall give the auditors reasonable access and assistance during normal business hours.

8.3 Each party shall bear its own costs of any audit, unless the audit reveals a material breach of this Agreement, in which case the audited party shall reimburse the Auditing Party's reasonable costs of that audit.

**9. NO LICENCE OR WARRANTY**

9.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party, and no licence of any intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement.

9.2 The Disclosing Party gives no warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to enter into any further contract.

**10. REMEDIES**

10.1 Without prejudice to any other rights or remedies, each party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the other party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief or specific performance.

**11. ANNOUNCEMENTS**

11.1 Neither party shall make any announcement concerning the Evaluation, or use the name or trade marks of the other party or its Group, without the prior written consent of the other party.

**12. GENERAL**

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the whole agreement between the parties relating to its subject matter.

12.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

12.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

12.4 Each party shall bear its own costs in connection with the negotiation and execution of this Agreement. If either party commences proceedings to enforce this Agreement and the other party is found to be in breach, the party in breach shall pay the enforcing party's costs of those proceedings (including legal costs on a full indemnity basis).

12.5 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or email to the addresses set out above.

12.6 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts and by electronic signature.

**13. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION**

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China.

13.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force when the Notice of Arbitration is submitted. The seat of arbitration shall be Hong Kong. The arbitration shall be conducted in English by one arbitrator.
