# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**THIS AGREEMENT** is dated ______________ 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **THAMESGATE COMMERCE LTD**, incorporated and registered in England and Wales with company number 11874523, whose registered office is at 4th Floor, 22 Bishopsgate Row, London EC2N 4AJ, United Kingdom (**Thamesgate**); and

**BACKGROUND**

(A) Thamesgate is a retail technology consultancy and reseller with clients in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

(B) The parties wish to discuss a possible partnership under which Thamesgate would introduce and implement Yunzhan's member-engagement platform for retail clients in the United Kingdom and Ireland (the **Potential Partnership**).

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Interpretation**

1.1 **Confidential Information** means any information that is confidential in nature, in whatever form it is recorded or kept, which a party or its Representatives (the **Discloser**) discloses to the other party or its Representatives (the **Recipient**) in connection with the Potential Partnership, including information relating to products, prices, plans, customers, suppliers, software and know-how.

1.2 **Representatives** means, in relation to a party, its employees, officers, contractors, subcontractors and professional advisers, and those of its group companies.

1.3 Clause headings shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**2. Obligations of confidentiality**

2.1 The Recipient shall keep the Discloser's Confidential Information secret and use it only to evaluate and negotiate the Potential Partnership.

2.2 The Recipient may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who need to know it for that purpose, provided that it informs them of its confidential nature before disclosure and procures that they comply with obligations equivalent to those in this Agreement.

2.3 The Recipient shall establish and maintain adequate security measures to safeguard the Confidential Information from unauthorised access or use, and in any event no less than the measures it applies to its own confidential information.

**3. Exceptions**

3.1 Clause 2 shall not apply to Confidential Information which:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, except through the Recipient's breach of this Agreement;

(b) was available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by the Discloser;

(c) was, is or becomes available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis from a person who is not bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Discloser; or

(d) is developed by or for the Recipient independently of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**4. Compelled disclosure**

4.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, by any governmental or other regulatory authority or by a court of competent jurisdiction, provided that, to the extent permitted by law, it gives the Discloser as much notice of the disclosure as possible.

**5. Personal data**

5.1 No personal data shall be shared between the parties under this Agreement. Any sharing of personal data in connection with the Potential Partnership shall be subject to a separate data processing agreement.

**6. No licence**

6.1 All Confidential Information shall remain the property of the Discloser, and no rights or licences in respect of it are granted to the Recipient other than as expressly set out in this Agreement.

**7. Duration**

7.1 This Agreement lasts for two (2) years from its date; either party may end it earlier by giving one (1) month's written notice.

7.2 The obligations in clauses 2 and 8 shall continue for five (5) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

**8. Return and destruction**

8.1 If so requested by the Discloser at any time by notice in writing, the Recipient shall promptly destroy or return to the Discloser all documents and materials containing the Discloser's Confidential Information, provided that the Recipient shall not be required to purge copies held in automatic electronic backup or archival systems, which shall remain subject to this Agreement until deleted in the ordinary course of business.

8.2 The Recipient shall confirm in writing to the Discloser when it has complied with clause 8.1.

**9. No warranty and no obligation**

9.1 The Discloser does not make any warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information.

9.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to continue discussions or to enter into the Potential Partnership.

13.4 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original, and may be signed electronically.
