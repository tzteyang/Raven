## 使用说明

1. **三种写法。** 每个条目分"首选写法（P）""底线写法（F）"，个别条目另有"限法务总监（D）"。修改意见先提 P；对方不接受，才在 F 以内回退；D 只有法务总监可以决定使用。
2. **审批人。** F 一栏写明谁能批准接受：写"合同组"的，合同组可以直接接受；写"法务经理"的，提交升级单，批准前不得向对方表示接受；写"无"的，表示没有回退余地，对方坚持即提交法务总监。
3. **对外怎么用。** 写进《修改意见（对外）》的只能是 P 的英文原文（按对方文本的条款号、定义词微调，例如把 Recipient 改成对方文本里用的 Receiving Party），不写 F、D，不写审批人。F 只在对方还价、并且已经按审批人一栏获得批准后才告诉对方。
4. **识别要点。** 每个条目附"对方常见写法"，摘自过往对方文本，帮助认出换了说法的同类条款。认条款看实质，不看标题。

## 2.1 双向性

- **P**：This Agreement is mutual. Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and the obligations of a Receiving Party under this Agreement apply equally to each Party in respect of the Confidential Information it receives.
- **F**：单向、只保护对方的文本按原文接受。前提是业务方书面确认合作中我方不披露任何保密信息。审批人：法务经理。
- 对方常见写法：the "Recipient" shall mean Yunzhan only; "Company" (the counterparty) as sole "Disclosing Party".

## 2.2 保密信息的定义

- **P**："Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, whether in writing, orally, electronically or by demonstration, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature and the circumstances of its disclosure.
- **F**：保留对方的标注要求，口头披露在 30 日内书面确认。审批人：合同组。

## 2.3 除外情形

- **P**（补回缺少的项，字母按对方文本顺延）：
  - (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
  - (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
  - (c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
  - (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
- **F**：措辞不同但实质相同的，接受。审批人：合同组。(d) 项不接受删除，没有回退，对方坚持即提交法务总监。

## 2.4 使用限制与知悉范围

- **P**：The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and may disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any such person.
- **F**：对方要求外包人员另签书面保密承诺的，接受。审批人：合同组。

## 2.5 期限与存续

- **P**：The obligations of the Receiving Party under this Agreement shall survive for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement, provided that, with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, such obligations shall continue for so long as such information remains a trade secret.
- **F**：存续期 5 年（five (5) years），商业秘密例外照旧。审批人：法务经理。超过 5 年或没有确定终点的，提交法务总监。
- 对方常见写法：shall survive for seven (7) years; shall continue in perpetuity; shall survive termination indefinitely; until such Confidential Information becomes publicly available; for so long as the information remains confidential; sixty (60) months after disclosure。

## 2.6 残留信息

- **P**：Delete Section [●] in its entirety. 需要时加一句：For clarity, nothing in this Agreement permits the Receiving Party to use or disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information on the ground that it has been retained in the memory of any individual.
- **F**：无。对方坚持保留的，提交法务总监。
- **D（仅限法务总监批准后使用，合同组不得主动提出，不得写进修改意见或交给业务方）**：Each Party's personnel may use general ideas, concepts and know-how of a non-specific nature that are retained in their unaided memory, provided that this right (i) does not extend to any source code, product roadmap, unreleased feature, pricing, customer data or personal information of the other Party, (ii) does not apply to information that has been deliberately memorised for the purpose of retaining it, and (iii) does not grant any licence under the other Party's patents, copyrights or trade secrets.
- 对方常见写法：
  - Residuals. Each party shall be free to use for any purpose the residuals resulting from access to or work with the Confidential Information of the other party.
  - Neither party shall have any obligation to limit or restrict the assignment of such persons or to pay royalties for any work resulting from the use of residuals.
  - The term "residuals" means information in non-tangible form which may be retained in the unaided memory of persons who have had access to the Confidential Information, including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques contained therein.
  - Recipient may use any information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel, including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques.
  - Nothing in this Agreement shall restrict either party from using the mental impressions of its personnel.
  - 不属于本项：Nothing in this Agreement prevents a party's personnel from using their general knowledge, skills and experience, provided that this does not permit the use or disclosure of the other party's Confidential Information.

## 2.7 返还与销毁

- **P**：..., provided that the Receiving Party shall not be required to purge copies held in automatic electronic backup or archival systems, which shall remain subject to this Agreement until deleted in the ordinary course of business.
- **F**：对方在还价中要求限定备份保留期的，备份按正常周期覆盖但不超过 12 个月可以接受；对方要求高管书面证明的，改为由经办负责人书面确认。返还销毁的期限本身不是问题（立场表 2.7），对方写几天都不必还价。审批人：合同组。
- 对方常见写法：destroy all copies, including copies held in any back-up or archive system; certify destruction by an officer within five (5) business days。

## 2.8 依法披露

- **F**：对方要求协助申请保护令、费用由对方承担的，接受。审批人：合同组。

General solicitations not specifically targeted at such employees (including public job advertisements and recruiter searches), and hiring any person who responds to them, shall not breach this Section. 审批人：法务经理。超过 12 个月、及于全部员工、或包含"不得雇用"的，都超出底线，提交法务总监。

## 2.12 个人信息

- **P**：The Parties do not intend to disclose personal information under this Agreement. Any disclosure of personal information between the Parties shall take place only under a separate written data processing agreement entered into in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
- **F**：无。合作中确需提供个人信息的，另签数据处理协议，怎么安排由法务经理决定；对方坚持把个人信息写进 NDA 的，提交法务总监。
- 对方常见写法：Confidential Information includes customer data, end-user data and personal data; Recipient may process personal data disclosed hereunder for the Purpose。

## 2.13 知识产权与反馈

- **P**：All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.
- **F**：对方可以使用我方对其产品的反馈意见，但不得借此使用或披露我方保密信息。审批人：合同组。

## 2.14 禁令救济

- **P**：Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm, and that the other Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available to it.
- **F**：措辞不同但双方对等的，接受。审批人：合同组。
