## 使用说明

1. **什么时候用我方模板。** 对方没有自己的文本，或对方文本的问题多到逐条修改比换文本还费事（一般是 RED，或三条以上 YELLOW），合同组可以在修改意见里建议改用我方模板。
2. **业务方可以直接填写的地方**：生效日期、对方名称和地址、合作目的（Purpose）的一句话描述、通知地址、签字栏。填写后无须再审，OA 签署审批时附"我方模板，未改动"的说明即可。
3. **改动须经合同组的地方**：除上一条以外的任何文字。对方在我方模板上做的修改，按《NDA 审查立场表》只看改动处。
4. **争议解决条款二选一**：对方是境外公司、我方由云栈科技（香港）有限公司签署的，用 Option A；对方是境内公司的，用 Option B。
5. 本模板的每一条都符合立场表 V2.3；条款号是我方模板自己的编号，审对方文本时不要拿来当对方的条款号。

## 英文正文

**MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (this "Agreement") is made on [date] (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) [Hangzhou Yunzhan Technology Co., Ltd., a company incorporated in the People's Republic of China, whose registered office is at Yunzhan Tower, No. 11 Xiyuan 8th Road, Xihu District, Hangzhou, China] / [Yunzhan Technology (Hong Kong) Limited, a company incorporated in Hong Kong, whose registered office is at Suite 2102, 21/F, Kingsgate Tower, 88 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong] ("Yunzhan"); and

(2) [Counterparty name], a company incorporated in [jurisdiction], whose registered office is at [address] (the "Counterparty"),

each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

The Parties wish to evaluate and discuss [describe the proposed cooperation in one sentence] (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The Parties therefore agree as follows.

**1. Definitions**

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, whether in writing, orally, electronically or by demonstration, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature and the circumstances of its disclosure, including product roadmaps, pricing, technical documentation, source code and business plans.

**2. Obligations**

2.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to its and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any such person.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

**3. Exclusions**

The obligations in Clause 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**4. Disclosure Required by Law**

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or order of a competent court or authority to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt notice and disclose only that part of the Confidential Information it is required to disclose.

**5. Personal Information**

The Parties do not intend to disclose personal information under this Agreement. Any disclosure of personal information between the Parties shall take place only under a separate written data processing agreement entered into in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

**6. No Use for Model Training**

Neither Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information to train, fine-tune, evaluate or otherwise improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

**7. Return and Destruction**

On the Disclosing Party's written request, or on expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, provided that the Receiving Party shall not be required to purge copies held in automatic electronic backup or archival systems, which shall remain subject to this Agreement until deleted in the ordinary course of business.

**8. Term and Survival**

8.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless terminated earlier by either Party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

8.2 The obligations of the Receiving Party under this Agreement shall survive for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement, provided that, with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, such obligations shall continue for so long as such information remains a trade secret.

**9. Ownership; No Licence; No Warranty**

9.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

9.2 Confidential Information is provided "as is", without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

9.3 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement or prevents either Party from working with third parties on similar matters, provided that it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information.

**10. Injunctive Relief**

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm, and that the other Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available to it.

**11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution**

Option A (Counterparty outside mainland China): This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force when the Notice of Arbitration is submitted. The seat of arbitration shall be Hong Kong and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

Option B (Counterparty in mainland China): This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China (for the purpose of this Agreement excluding Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan). Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the Shanghai International Arbitration Center for arbitration in Shanghai in accordance with its rules then in effect. The arbitral award shall be final and binding on both Parties.

**12. General**

12.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions about it.

12.2 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor in a merger or a sale of substantially all of its relevant business.

12.4 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses set out above or as notified in writing.

12.5 Neither Party shall use the other Party's name or logo in any publicity without its prior written consent.

12.6 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
