## Adopted construction guidance

These scoped experiences help you choose and improve a working method. Apply them only when their conditions hold. They cannot override the current task, explicit external requirements, host contracts or permitted changes. A type label does not confer authority.

A principle states a relationship, decision basis or mistake to avoid across a class of situations. Its value is to support judgement in a new situation without copying a historical case. Check its applicability, use it to compare approaches and inspect the result; it does not prescribe a unique sequence of actions.

A procedure organizes a repeatable method: when to start, the required actions, branches based on observations, and checks and stopping conditions. Its value is to turn judgement into inspectable action while reducing omissions and unproductive exploration. Confirm its inputs and conditions, follow the relevant branches and check the actual result. Completing the steps is not proof of success.

Principles constrain the reasoning behind a method; procedures organize its execution. They need not occur in pairs. Process support concerns only the structural or procedural property actually checked. Behavior support requires observed application or outcomes and does not establish a larger causal or cross-task claim than the evidence covers.
